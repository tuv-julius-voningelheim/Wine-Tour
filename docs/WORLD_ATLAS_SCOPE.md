# Vine Atlas — broad world coverage

This is the required geographic and producer seed for the first deployment. It is intentionally broad. A small set of “deep editorial” records has long-form terroir and winemaking content; every entry here must still have coordinates, a concise original summary, signature grapes/styles, at least one notable producer and source provenance.

The list uses consumer-recognisable region groupings. Protected appellations and nested subregions remain separate linked records where they materially help learning. Producer inclusion is educational, not a ranking or endorsement.

## Data-depth rule

- Tier A: 20 deep regions and 30 deep producers — long-form terroir, history, subregions, signature wines, related knowledge and primary sources.
- Tier B: all remaining entries — concise original profile, coordinates, climate/soil tags, signature grapes/styles, linked producers, related regions and at least one authoritative source.
- Community/admin records never silently overwrite curated records. Provenance and review date remain visible.

## Visible-copy acceptance rule

Every region, producer, wine, grape and aroma shown in the interface must have publishable editorial copy. Internal planning terms, draft instructions, prompts, template markers, “AI” phrasing and placeholder UI are release blockers. The public interface may expose editorial provenance and sources, but never the drafting process.
