Prompt: sensory reference bench for oxidation, reduction, cork taint, volatile acidity, light exposure and clean control beside a top-down acid, salt, fat, heat, sweetness and umami balance study; no text or branded objects.

Prompt: warm ivory archival paper with five language-neutral evidence stations for cork taint, oxidation, reduction, volatile acidity and leathery/barnyard deviation; restrained ink and watercolor, glass silhouettes, no text, logos or copied diagram.
